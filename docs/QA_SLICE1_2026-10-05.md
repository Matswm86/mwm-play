# MWM Play slice 1: QA report (2026-10-05)

**Verdict: NEEDS-CHANGES.** Nothing blocks: lint passes, there are no script errors, the screens are not broken and CI is green. The shell is solid. The problem is the first task: **Ball Connect level 1 is not a 4-year-old's task.** It has 6 pairs, and every pair's straight line is crossed by another pair. Water Sort level 1 is fine to play, but its targets break rules 1, 6, 7 and 15. Do not hand this to a child as "ready" until Ball Connect's first level is replaced.

Tested build: working tree at `5cc0108`. During this QA the repo was squashed to `70e8616` and pushed (public). Diffing my tested copy against `70e8616` `app/` shows one difference only: a comment in `shell/PerfOverlay.gd`. So every result below applies to `70e8616`.

Evidence folder (my own runs): a local scratch folder (not in this repo). It holds the `walk/`, `walk2/`, `walk3/`, `layout_1080x2400/` and `layout_1200x1920/` shots, the logs, and the bot `qa_runner.gd`. The bot runs real touch events, starts from an empty user dir (`XDG_DATA_HOME`), and ran on a scratch copy of `app/` with `--audio-driver Dummy` under Xvfb on the RTX 3060.

Units: mm = px x 25.4 / dpi. At 400 dpi 1 mm = 15.75 px; at 430 dpi 1 mm = 16.93 px. Rule 1 (12.7 mm) therefore needs 200 px at 400 dpi and 215 px at 430 dpi.

## Findings

Severity: HIGH = fix before a child plays; MED = fix before Play Store; LOW = note.

1. **HIGH: Ball Connect level 1 is too hard for the first task.** `app/games/ball-connect/data/levels/level_01.json` has 6 pairs, and each pair is crossed by another pair's straight line: red and blue form an X, green (vertical) crosses yellow (horizontal), and orange and pink form an X. Evidence:
   - Screenshot `qa/walk/02_bc_level1_cold.png`.
   - A child's first move (drag red straight to red) draws nothing. Log: `BC straight red drag: pairs=0`, shot `qa/walk/04_bc_straight_red_drag_result.png`. The line stops at the first blocking ball, with no cue.
   - My grid router (BFS, 20 px grid) linked green, yellow and red, then found `no route for blue/orange/pink` in 3 orders (`qa/walk.log`; state in `qa/walk/05_bc_guard.png`). A finer 12 px router timed out after 450 s.
   - I did **not** prove level 1 is solvable inside the screen. The win frames below are from a forced win.
   - No idle hint after 8 s (`BC after 8 s idle: hint visible=false`, rule 18).

   Change (game-designer, upstream repo): the first task needs 1-2 pairs with no crossing, then add crossings over later levels.
2. **HIGH: Ball Connect ball start target is below rule 1.** The ball touch radius on screen is 65.5-68.2 px (log `BC ball ... touch_radius_px`). The diameter of 131-136 px is 8.3-8.7 mm at 400 dpi and 7.7-8.0 mm at 430 dpi; rule 1 needs 12.7 mm. A drag only starts inside the drawn radius (`games/ball-connect/scripts/Ball.gd:19`, `LineDrawer.gd:59-63`); the end snap is 1.6x. Change: start hit radius about 1.6x too (rule 5, rule 12).
3. **HIGH: Ball Connect "Reset" button breaks rules 1, 3 and 7.** Rect (830,66) 210x90 px (`GameManager.gd:200-204`) is 13.3 x 5.7 mm at 400 dpi and 12.4 x 5.3 mm at 430 dpi. That is under the 6.35 mm floor of rule 3. It is inset 40 px from the right edge and 66 px from the top, so it does not reach the edge (rule 7). It is an English word with no icon. Screenshot `qa/walk/02_bc_level1_cold.png`.
4. **MED: Water Sort targets break rules 1, 6, 7 and 15.** Measured rects (`qa/walk.log`, code `Game.gd:520-527`, `IconButton.gd:16`):
   - Undo (340,1690) 130x130 and +1 Bottle (580,1690) 170x130 sit in the wrist strip (y >= 1664), which breaks rule 6. On 20:9 they are at y 2170-2300 against a strip at y >= 2144 (`qa/layout_1080x2400.log`).
   - Restart (766,130) and sound (916,130) are 130x130 px each: 8.3 mm at 400 dpi and 7.7 mm at 430 dpi, under rule 1. Sound is inset 34 px from the right and 130 px from the top, which breaks rule 7.
   - `WsIconButton` fires on touch-**down** (`IconButton.gd:21-24`). Rule 15 wants the action on release, so a palm resting on +1 Bottle spends the extra bottle.
   - Bottle hit rect is 158 x 437 px = 10.1 x 27.7 mm at 400 dpi and 9.3 x 25.8 mm at 430 dpi. The width is under rule 1.
   - "Next" on the win panel is 440 x 150 px = 27.9 x 9.5 mm at 400 dpi. Its height is under rule 1.
5. **MED: Water Sort's own sound button gives a child a mute that the parent area cannot undo.** `Game.gd:552-560` saves `sound=false` to `user://water-sort_save.cfg`. The parent "Musikk" and "Lydeffekter" toggles only mute the shell buses, so they cannot turn it back on. Change: hide the game's sound button inside the shell (adapter), or map it to the shell settings.
6. **MED: Ball Connect's bottom balls reach into the wrist strip (rule 6).** The bottom row centres at y 1612 with a 68 px radius, so the touch area runs to y 1680, 16 px into the strip (y >= 1664). Log `qa/walk.log`.
7. **MED: Game text is English, and the child screens show words a non-reader cannot use.** Examples: "Level 1", "Reset", "Undo", "+1 Bottle", "Level 1 complete / Tap to continue" (`GameManager.gd:104`), "Level %d complete!" and "Next" (`Game.gd:405, 490`). Screenshots `qa/walk/15_ws_win_5s.png` and `qa/walk3/16_bc_win_5s.png`. DESIGN section 3 says there is no text in child buttons. Within 3 s a non-reader can still find "Next", since it is the only big green shape. In Ball Connect, the cue "tap anywhere to continue" is text-only.
8. **MED: The non-reader cues in DESIGN are not built yet.** Missing: the spoken game name on tile release (DESIGN 2a), the "Hent en voksen" voice on the gate (2c), and the idle hint (rule 18). Voice is blocked on recording (DESIGN 7.5). Until it exists, the start screen works by picture only. That is fine for 2 tiles.
9. **LOW: The leave guard is easy for "tap, tap".** Two ordinary taps released 612 ms and 1076 ms after start left the game (`qa/walk3.log`: `HOME two back-to-back taps ... screen=start`). Rule 10 allows this, as designed. One tap followed by 2.8 s of nothing stays in the game (`HOME after 2.8 s no second tap: screen=game`). The guard ring is visible in `qa/walk/05_bc_guard.png`.
10. **LOW: Holdover filter (rule 8) not proven at runtime.** Frames under Xvfb took 100-600 ms (`qa/walk3.log` frame timings), so the 300 ms window could not be hit; all four holdover tries launched the game at 334 ms or later. By code review the filter is correct: `Shell.gd:243-252` marks touch and mouse events as handled for 300 ms after every `_change_scene`. Check it on the phone.
11. **LOW: Play-limit stop can land 1.5 s into the next level.** Once `_stop_timer` starts it never re-checks `is_at_stopping_point` (`Shell.gd:404-413`). A child who taps "continue" within 1.5 s gets pulled out of a fresh board. Harmless, since nothing is lost.
12. **LOW: Progress keeps the level, not the board (rule 28).** Ball Connect saved `{"current_level":1,...}` on leave. Water Sort reopened at "Level 2" after the relaunch (`qa/walk2.log`). Pause and resume kept the board and music (`PAUSE/RESUME: ... tubes kept=true`). A half-solved board is lost on leave or kill in both games. Acceptable for rule 28 "quitting costs nothing" at these sizes, but note it.
13. **LOW: PerfOverlay toggles on any 3 simultaneous touches** (`shell/PerfOverlay.gd:24-33`). A child's palm can show a yellow debug readout. Consider removing it from release builds.
14. **LOW: Privacy text describes billing that does not exist yet.** `shell/screens/Privacy.gd:36` says "connects to the internet only to let Google Play confirm a purchase", but the APK has no INTERNET permission and no billing. Fine for sideload; re-check when billing lands (rule 44).

## Kids walk-through (non-reader, sound off, cold start)

| Step | What the child sees | Gets stuck? |
|---|---|---|
| Cold start | 2 large picture tiles, gear top-right (`qa/walk/01_start_cold.png`). Nothing in the bottom strip | No. Tiles are 484x456 px hit = 30.7x29.0 mm at 400 dpi (28.6x26.9 at 430) |
| Tap Ball Connect | 12 balls, "Level 1", "Reset" (`02_bc_level1_cold.png`) | **Yes.** Straight drags are blocked by other balls, with no cue (finding 1) |
| Leave | Home disc top-left; tap gives ring; second tap gives start (`05_bc_guard.png`, `06_start_after_bc.png`) | No. Hit area is 216x216 to the corner = 13.7 mm at 400 dpi, 12.8 mm at 430 (rules 1 and 7 pass) |
| Tap Water Sort | 3 bottles, red and orange, 5-move solve (`07_ws_level1_cold.png`) | Probably not: tap-tap is guessable by trial |
| Win | "Level 1 complete!" plus a green "Next"; still waiting after 5 s (`15_ws_win_5s.png`) | No; no autoplay (rule 26 pass, both games) |
| Accidental taps | Undo and +1 Bottle sit under the palm in the strip and fire on touch-down; the sound button mutes the game for good; "Reset" in Ball Connect | Findings 4, 5, 3 |

**Parent area by accident:** 1 tap on the gear (160x160 px hit = 10.2 mm at 400 dpi) opens the gate (`qa/walk2/02_gate_child.png`). Past that, a child needs the exact 3-digit code. My calc: about 1/1000 per 3-press attempt (1/1331 counting the delete key). 600 random key presses did not get in (`GATE random child presses: 600 screen=gate`). One home tap returns to start. Steps to the parent area: 1 tap plus decoding the code, so not reachable by a pre-reader in practice.

**Back during scene change:** a tile tap then back x2 within 2 frames left the app in the game with the guard ring showing. The second back fell inside the 300 ms floor and was ignored (`BACKx2 during launch: screen=game ... active=water-sort`, `qa/walk/17_back_during_launch.png`). There was no crash and no stuck state. Back on the start screen calls `send_to_background()`. On desktop that quits, which ended run 1. `AndroidRuntime.getActivity().moveTaskToBack` is unverified on a device.
