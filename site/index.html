MWM Play

One Android app with puzzle and play games for children and families, with no ads and no tracking.

You can try every game for free. A parent can unlock all of them with one payment, including games added later. There is no subscription.

Coming to Google Play. Not released yet.

What is in it

  • Ball Connect: drag a line between balls of the same colour, without crossing other lines.
  • Water Sort: pour coloured water between bottles until each bottle holds one colour.
  • Tile Explorer: pick 3D tiles into a tray and match three of the same picture to clear the board.
  • Spotless: wash and restore dirty old things, like a mossy sink or a muddy car, until they look new.
  • Timber Valley: run a calm lumber mill: chop trees, saw planks, sell them and build new machines.
  • Brick Nova and Ridge Riders: new games in development.

What the app does not do

  • No ads.
  • No tracking and no data collected.
  • No chat and no links out of the app.
  • No purchases without a parent.
  • Works offline.

Who makes it

MWM Play is made in Norway by a parent and teacher with more than ten years in classrooms and kindergartens.
