MWM Play

Privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. The MWM Play app does not collect, store or send any personal data to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on the device

Game progress, such as finished levels and best times, is saved only on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see card details or the buyer's name. Each purchase sits behind a parent gate in the app.

Children

The app is made for children. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Internet access

The app connects to the internet only to let Google Play confirm a purchase. The games work offline.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
